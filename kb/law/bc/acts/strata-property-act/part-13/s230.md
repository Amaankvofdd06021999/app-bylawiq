# Section 230 — Annual general meeting after deposit of subsequent phase

Subject to the regulations, if a phase other than the first phase of a phased strata plan is deposited, the strata corporation must hold an annual general meeting during the 6 week period that begins on the earlier of

- (a) the date on which 50% plus one of the strata lots in the new phase have been conveyed to purchasers, and
- (b) the date that is 6 months after the deposit of the new phase.
