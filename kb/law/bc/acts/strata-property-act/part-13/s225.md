# Section 225 — Approving officer's approval for common facilities

## (1)

If a common facility is to be constructed in conjunction with a phase of a strata plan, the phase must be approved by an approving officer in accordance with the regulations.

## (2)

The approving officer must approve the phase if

- (a) the owner developer fulfills the requirements of section 223, or
- (b) the common facility is at least 50% completed, as verified by the certificate of a registered architect or professional engineer.
