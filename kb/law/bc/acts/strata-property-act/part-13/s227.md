# Section 227 — Owner developer's contribution to expenses

## (1)

Subject to sections 233 (2) and 235 (3), until all phases of a phased strata plan have been deposited, the owner developer must contribute to the expenses of the strata corporation that are attributable to the common facilities.

## (3)

For the purposes of subsection (2), the unit entitlement of strata lots in the phases not deposited is as set out in the Phased Strata Plan Declaration.
