# Section 238 — Building permit

## (1)

An application for a building permit for a phase in a phased strata plan must include the common facilities for the phase as set out in the Phased Strata Plan Declaration.

## (2)

Parcels in a phased strata plan that will be consolidated under section 228 (1) (a) on the deposit of a phase are deemed to be consolidated for the purpose of enabling a building inspector to issue a building permit in respect of a building.
