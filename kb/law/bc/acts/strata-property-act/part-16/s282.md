# Section 282 — Approval of disposition

## (1)

Before any land or personal property is disposed of, the liquidator must obtain the approval of the disposition by a resolution passed by a 3/4 vote at an annual or special general meeting, or the disposition is void.

## (2)

The resolution may specify the conditions under which a disposition may be made.
