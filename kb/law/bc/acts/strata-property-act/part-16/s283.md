# Section 283 — Filing of application for dissolution

The registrar must not file the application for dissolution referred to in section 343 (1) of the *Business Corporations Act* as it applies for the purposes of this Act unless that application is accompanied by a Certificate of Strata Corporation in the prescribed form stating that the final accounts referred to in the application have been approved by a resolution passed by a 3/4 vote at an annual or special general meeting.
