# Section 280 — Filing vesting order

## (1)

The liquidator must deliver a certified copy of the vesting order under section 279, accompanied by the interest schedule, to the registrar.

## (2)

The registrar must file the order and interest schedule if satisfied that

- (a) the legal description of the land in the interest schedule is sufficient to allow the registrar to identify it in the records of the land title office, and
- (b) the liquidator will have a good, safeholding and marketable title to the land.

## (3)

When the order is filed, the liquidator must notify the owners, registered charge holders and other creditors identified in the interest schedule by registered mail to their addresses given in that schedule.
