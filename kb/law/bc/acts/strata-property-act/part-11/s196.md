# Section 196 — Administration of section

## (1)

The eligible voters of a section may call and hold meetings and pass resolutions in the same manner as eligible voters of the strata corporation.

## (2)

Each section must elect an executive for that section and the section executive has the same powers and duties with respect to the section as the strata corporation's council has with respect to the strata corporation.

## (3)

A member of a section executive is eligible for election to the strata corporation's council.
