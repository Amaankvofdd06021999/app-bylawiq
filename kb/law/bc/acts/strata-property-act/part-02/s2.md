# Section 2 — Establishment of strata corporation

## (1)

From the time the strata plan is deposited in a land title office,

- (a) a strata corporation is established, and
- (b) the owners of the strata lots in the strata plan are members of the strata corporation under the name "The Owners, Strata Plan *[the registration number of the strata plan]*".

## (2)

Subject to any limitation under this Act, a strata corporation has the power and capacity of a natural person of full capacity.
