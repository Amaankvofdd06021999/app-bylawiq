# Section 203 — Conversion of ground lease

## (1)

The deposit of the leasehold strata plan operates as a conversion of the ground lease into individual leases of the leasehold landlord's interest in each strata lot, including its share in the common property,

- (a) in the form of the model strata lot lease attached to the ground lease, and
- (b) granted to the owner developer by the leasehold landlord.

## (2)

The conversion of the ground lease does not affect the obligations of the owner developer and the leasehold landlord under the ground lease.
