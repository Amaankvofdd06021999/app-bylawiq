# Section 215 — Cancellation of strata plan

## (1)

On termination of all of the strata lot leases, the leasehold strata plan must be cancelled and the strata corporation must be wound up in accordance with Part 16.

## (2)

A leasehold strata plan may only be cancelled and the strata corporation wound up on termination of all the strata lot leases.
