# Section 252 — Common property record

## (1)

On the deposit of a strata plan, the registrar must establish a common property record for the strata corporation.

## (2)

The common property record in subsection (1) forms part of the register.

## (3)

Despite any other provision of this Act or the *Land Title Act*, the registrar must note on the common property record a charge or other interest that separately charges the common property.
