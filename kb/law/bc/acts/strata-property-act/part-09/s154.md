# Section 154 — Review and report on insurance

The strata corporation must

- (a) review annually the adequacy of the strata corporation's insurance,
- (b) report on the insurance coverage at each annual general meeting, and
- (c) inform owners and tenants as soon as feasible of any material change in the strata corporation's insurance coverage, including any increase in an insurance deductible.
