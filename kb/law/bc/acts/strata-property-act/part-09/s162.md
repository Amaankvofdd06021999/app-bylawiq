# Section 162 — Contribution

Despite the terms of the insurance policy,

- (a) neither the strata corporation's insurance policy nor an owner's insurance policy is liable to be brought into contribution with another policy unless the other policy is issued on the same property, and
- (b) neither the strata corporation's insurance policy nor the owner's insurance policy is "other insurance" in relation to another policy unless the other policy is issued on the same property.
