# Section 64 — Legal service on strata corporation

Despite section 63 but subject to another enactment or a court order, service on a strata corporation of a notice of a proceeding in any court may only be effected by

- (a) personal service on a council member, or
- (b) mailing it, by registered mail, to the strata corporation at its most recent mailing address on file in the land title office.
