# Section 65 — Informing resident owners and tenants

For the purposes of sections 98 (6), 106, 108 (4), 125 (4), 128 (4) and 167, and any regulations that require the strata corporation to inform owners or tenants of certain matters, the strata corporation may, instead of giving notice under section 61, inform resident owners and tenants by one or more of the following methods or by any other method:

- (a) leaving a document containing the information at a location designated by the strata corporation for the distribution of such information;
- (b) posting a document containing the information in a part of the common property designated by the strata corporation for the posting of such information.
