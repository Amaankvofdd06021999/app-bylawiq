# Section 34 — Approval of council member remuneration

Any remuneration paid to a member of council for the member's exercise of council powers or performance of council duties must be approved in advance of payment

- (a) in the budget,
- (b) in the bylaws, or
- (c) by a resolution passed by a 3/4 vote at an annual or special general meeting.
