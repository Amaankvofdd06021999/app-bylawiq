# Section 178.1 — Effect of tribunal proceeding on arbitration

## (1)

Subject to subsection (2), a dispute must not be referred to arbitration under section 177 (1) or (2) of this Act once an initiating notice has been given under section 6 of the *Civil Resolution Tribunal Act* in relation to the dispute.

## (2)

A dispute may be referred to arbitration under section 177 (1) or (2) if the civil resolution tribunal notifies the parties of its refusal to resolve the dispute.
