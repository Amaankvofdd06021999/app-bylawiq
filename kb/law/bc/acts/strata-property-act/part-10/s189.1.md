# Section 189.1 — Strata corporations, owners and tenants initiating tribunal proceeding

## (1)

Subject to subsection (2), a strata corporation, owner or tenant may make a request under section 4 of the *Civil Resolution Tribunal Act* asking the civil resolution tribunal to resolve a dispute concerning any strata property matter over which the civil resolution tribunal has jurisdiction.

## (2)

An owner or tenant may not make a request referred to in subsection (1) unless

- (a) the owner or tenant requested a council hearing under section 34.1, or
- (b) the civil resolution tribunal, on request by the strata corporation, owner or tenant, directs that the requirements of paragraph (a) of this subsection do not apply.
