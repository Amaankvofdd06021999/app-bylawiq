# Section 163 — Strata corporation may be sued

## (1)

The strata corporation may be sued as representative of the owners with respect to any matter relating to the common property, common assets, bylaws or rules, or involving an act or omission of the strata corporation.

## (2)

An owner may sue the strata corporation.
