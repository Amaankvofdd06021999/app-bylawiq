# Section 189.4 — Suit requirements and procedures apply

The requirements and procedures regarding authorization for suits and liability for expenses and judgments in suits by and against the strata corporation, as set out in the following sections, apply to a dispute being resolved by the civil resolution tribunal in which the strata corporation is a party:

- (a) section 166 *[owner's liability for judgment against corporation]*;
- (b) section 167 *[defending suits]*;
- (c) section 169 *[limit on owner's responsibility for costs]*;
- (d) section 171 (5) and (6) *[corporation may sue as representative of all owners]*;
- (e) section 172 (1) (a), (2) and (3) *[corporation may sue on behalf of some owners]*.
