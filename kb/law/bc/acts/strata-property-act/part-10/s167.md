# Section 167 — Defending suits

## (1)

The strata corporation must inform owners as soon as feasible if it is sued.

## (2)

The expense of defending a suit brought against the strata corporation is shared by the owners in the same manner as a judgment is shared under section 166, except that an owner who is suing the strata corporation is not required to contribute.
