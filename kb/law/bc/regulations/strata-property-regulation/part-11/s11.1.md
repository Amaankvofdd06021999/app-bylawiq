# Section 11.1 — Different types of residential strata lots for the purpose of creating sections

For the purposes of section 191 (1) (c) of the Act, the following are the different types of residential strata lots:

- (a) apartment-style strata lots;
- (b) townhouse-style strata lots;
- (c) detached houses.
