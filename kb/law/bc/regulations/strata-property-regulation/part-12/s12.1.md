# Section 12.1 — Definitions for section 199 of the Act

For the purposes of the definition of "leasehold landlord" in section 199 of the Act, **"public authority"** means any of the following:

- (a) a university as defined in the *University Act*;
- (b) the shíshálh Nation established under section 5 (1) of the *shíshálh Nation Self-Government Act* (Canada);
- (c) the Provincial Rental Housing Corporation;
- (d) a board as defined in section 1 of the *School Act*;
- (e) Musqueam Block F Land Ltd.;
- (f) MST (Marine Drive, WV) Land Holdings Ltd.;
- (g) MST (Fairmont) Land Holdings Ltd.;
- (h) 0996501 B.C. Ltd.

[am. B.C. Regs. 158/2015; 70/2019; 123/2024, Sch., s. 24; 172/2024.]
