# Section 17.8 — Certificate of Payment

## (1)

A certificate in Form A under section 38 of the *Condominium Act* issued by a strata corporation before the coming into force of this section is deemed to be a Certificate of Payment under section 115 of the Act.

## (2)

The certificate in Form A referred to in subsection (1) is valid for the purposes of section 256 of the Act for a period of 3 months from the date it was issued.
