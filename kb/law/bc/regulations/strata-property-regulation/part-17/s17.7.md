# Section 17.7 — Short term exclusive use

Despite section 76 (2) of the Act, a right, permission or privilege granted, before the coming into force of this section, under section 117 (f) of the *Condominium Act* or a similar bylaw under which the strata corporation gives an owner permission to exclusively use or enjoy, or a special privilege in relation to, common property that is not designated as limited common property under section 53 of the *Condominium Act* continues to be enforceable in accordance with its terms, but may be renewed only as permitted by section 76 of the Act.
