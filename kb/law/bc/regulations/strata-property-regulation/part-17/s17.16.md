# Section 17.16 — Arbitration

## (1)

If an arbitration has commenced under section 44 of the *Condominium Act* before the coming into force of this section,

- (a) section 45 of the *Condominium Act* applies to that arbitration, and
- (b) sections 175 to 189 of the Act do not apply to that arbitration.

## (2)

For the purposes of this section, an arbitration has commenced if the arbitrator or arbitrators have been appointed under section 44 of the *Condominium Act*.
