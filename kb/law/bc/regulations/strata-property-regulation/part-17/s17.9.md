# Section 17.9 — Payment of strata fees

If a strata corporation created under the *Condominium Act* has no bylaw that establishes a schedule for the payment of strata fees, the strata corporation is deemed, on the coming into force of this section, to have the bylaw set out in section 1 of the Standard Bylaws to the Act.
