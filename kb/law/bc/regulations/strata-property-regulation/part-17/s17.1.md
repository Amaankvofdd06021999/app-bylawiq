# Section 17.1 — Definitions

For the purposes of this Part:

**"Amended Schedule of Interest on Destruction"** means an Amended Schedule of Interest on Destruction prepared in Form Z.1 for filing with the registrar;

**"*Condominium Act*"** means the *Condominium Act*, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 64, or other former Act;

**"schedule of interest on destruction"** means, with respect to a strata plan, the schedule required by section 4 (g) of the *Condominium Act* or an Amended Schedule of Interest on Destruction, most recently filed with the registrar.

[en. B.C. Reg. 289/2000, s. 1.]
