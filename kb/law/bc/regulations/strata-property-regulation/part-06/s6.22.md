# Section 6.22 — Exemption from requirement to obtain depreciation report

Section 94 (2) of the Act does not apply in relation to a strata corporation if and for so long as there are fewer than 5 strata lots in the strata plan.

[en. B.C. Reg. 88/2024, Sch., s. 3.]
