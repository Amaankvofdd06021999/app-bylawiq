# Section 5.7 — Definitions for Division

In this Division:

**"existing strata corporation"** means a strata corporation established on or before December 31, 2023 by the deposit of

- (a) the first phase of a phased strata plan, or
- (b) a strata plan that is not a phased strata plan;

**"new strata corporation"** means a strata corporation established after December 31, 2023 by the deposit of

- (a) the first phase of a phased strata plan, or
- (b) a strata plan that is not a phased strata plan;

**"specified area"** means any of the following:

- (a) the Capital Regional District, other than an island within the Capital Regional District that is accessible only by air or boat;
- (b) the Fraser Valley Regional District;
- (c) the Metro Vancouver Regional District, other than an island that is accessible only by air or boat.

[en. B.C. Reg. 261/2023, App., s. 2.]
