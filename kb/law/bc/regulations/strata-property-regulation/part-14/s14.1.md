# Section 14.1 — Definition for sections 241 and 242 of the Act

For the purposes of sections 241 and 242 of the Act, **"previously occupied"** means occupied at any time in its past for any purpose, including residential, commercial, institutional, recreational or industrial use, but does not include the occupation of a proposed strata lot by the owner developer solely as a display lot for the sale of strata lots in the proposed strata plan.
