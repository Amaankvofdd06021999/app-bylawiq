# Section 14.9 — Application to deposit strata plan

Every strata plan tendered for deposit and registration must be accompanied by

- (a) unless the plan is in electronic format, an Application to Deposit Strata Plan in Form Z, signed by the person applying to deposit the strata plan or by the person's agent, and
- (b) the duplicate indefeasible title covering the land if it is not already in the land title office.

[en. B.C. Reg. 295/2006, s. 6; am. B.C. Reg. 64/2021, s. 3.]
