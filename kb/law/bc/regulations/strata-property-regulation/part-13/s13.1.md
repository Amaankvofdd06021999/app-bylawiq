# Section 13.1 — Fees for phased developments

If the land included in a phased development is in a rural area, there must be paid to the approving officer the following fees:

- (a) for examining a Phased Strata Plan Declaration in Form P under section 222 of the Act .................................................... $100
- (b) for approving in Form Q a strata plan under section 224 of the Act................................................................................... $100.
