# Bylaw pattern: smoking and cannabis

## The shapes this bylaw takes

Three shapes. A common property ban, prohibiting smoking on common property and limited common property including balconies and patios. A whole-property ban, extending into strata lots. And a grandfathering scheme, where a whole-property ban exempts residents who smoked before the bylaw passed, sometimes for as long as they live there and sometimes for a fixed period. Cannabis is usually folded into the smoking bylaw, with growing dealt with separately as a use or nuisance matter.

## What the Act and Regulation allow

The Act and the Regulation say nothing about smoking, vaping, tobacco or cannabis, so these bylaws rest entirely on the general bylaw power [bc.spa.s119] and on the standard use bylaw's nuisance, hazard and unreasonable interference grounds [bc.spa.sched.bylaw3], which is the provision smoke drift complaints are usually brought under. Two consequences follow. First, there is no statutory grandfathering for smokers: the Act's grandfathering provision is about pets and applies only to pets [bc.spa.s123], so any exemption for existing smokers exists only because the building's own bylaw says so, and is only as durable as that bylaw. Second, the *Human Rights Code* limit is the live one [bc.spa.s121], both for a resident who uses cannabis for a medical reason and for a resident whose health is affected by smoke drift; the kb cannot yet cite the Code (task A5). Amendments need a 3/4 vote and filing [bc.spa.s128].

## Where these bylaws go wrong

Assuming existing smokers are protected the way existing pets are; writing a grandfathering clause without saying whether it runs with the person or the strata lot; treating a medical cannabis authorisation as the end of the analysis in either direction; and enforcing smoke drift as a smoking contravention when the building has no smoking bylaw, rather than as nuisance or unreasonable interference under the standard use bylaw. As with every penalty, the complaint and answer process comes first [bc.spa.s135].
