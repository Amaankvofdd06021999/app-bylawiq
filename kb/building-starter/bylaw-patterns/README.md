# Bylaw patterns

Empty until research plan R7 fills it. One item per common bylaw amendment pattern, type `bylaw-pattern`, citing the law and decisions that shape it.

## What is here

One `bylaw-pattern` item per high-traffic topic, id `building.bylaw-pattern.<topic>`: rentals, short-term rentals, pets, noise, smoking and cannabis, parking, alterations and renovations, move-in and move-out, and fines. Each has four headings, which are the chunk boundaries:

- **The shapes this bylaw takes** — the structures a bylaw on this topic can have, and what turns on the difference between them.
- **What the Act and Regulation allow** — the provisions that authorise and bound each shape, cited.
- **Where these bylaws go wrong** — the failure modes, tied to the provision each one offends.
- **Tested in** — empty until Phase C adds decision summaries. Until the CRT and the courts answer the permission requests in `research/open-questions.md` (items 18 and 19), the kb holds no decision summaries at all, so no pattern can yet say what has been held about it.

The shapes are reasoned from what the legislation permits and forbids, not from a survey of filed bylaws. Confirming them against real registered bylaws is review work, and each item's `notes` says so. Every item is `draft` and needs counsel review.

Two items carry unresolved questions rather than answers: `rentals` and `short-term-rentals` both turn on whether section 141's bar on restricting the rental of a strata lot reaches short-term accommodation, which the Act and Regulation do not settle (open question 33).
