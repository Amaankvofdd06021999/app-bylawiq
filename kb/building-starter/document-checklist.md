# Documents to upload for a new building

BylawIQ answers from the building's own documents first. The more of these a building uploads, the fewer questions end in "no grounding found".

## Essential

- **Registered bylaws, with every filed amendment.** The consolidated set plus each amendment as filed with the Land Title Office. Answers about what the rules are come from these.
- **Rules made by council.** Current rules and the minutes where they were ratified.
- **Strata plan.** Shows strata lots, common property and limited common property.

## Strongly recommended

- **Minutes from the last two years.** Council and general meetings, including resolutions and votes.
- **Current budget and financial statements.**
- **Depreciation report**, or the resolution waiving it.
- **Insurance summary**, including deductibles.

## Useful

- **Engineering and building envelope reports.**
- **Contracts with the management company and major service providers.**
- **Past bylaw enforcement correspondence**, redacted as your privacy policy requires.
