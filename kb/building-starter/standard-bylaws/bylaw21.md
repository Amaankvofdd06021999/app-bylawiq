# Standard bylaw 21: who may spend the strata corporation's money

## What it covers

Bars anyone from spending the corporation's money without a delegated power to do so, but lets an individual council member spend it to repair or replace common property or common assets where the work is immediately required to ensure safety or prevent significant loss or damage [bc.spa.sched.bylaw21].

## When it applies to your building

Standard bylaw 21 is one of your building's bylaws unless, and to the extent that, a different bylaw on this subject is filed in the land title office [bc.spa.s120]. An owner developer may have filed different bylaws when the strata plan was deposited [bc.spa.s120], and owners may have amended them since by 3/4 vote and filing an Amendment to Bylaws; until that filing an amendment has no effect [bc.spa.s128]. So the question is always what the registered bylaws say, not what the standard bylaw says.

## What a building can change, and the limits

Buildings commonly add a dollar threshold above which council must get quotes or an owners' vote. The Act's spending rules sit above this bylaw and are not changed by it: expenditures from the contingency reserve fund [bc.spa.s96], from the operating fund [bc.spa.s97], and unapproved expenditures [bc.spa.s98] each have their own requirements. Delegation of spending power must meet standard bylaw 20's form [bc.spa.sched.bylaw20].

## What to check in the registered bylaws

Any spending limits in the registered bylaws and whether council observes them; and whether emergency spending under this bylaw is recorded and reported, since it is the exception owners scrutinise.
