# Standard bylaw 30: the owner developer's display lot

## What it covers

Lets an owner developer with an unsold strata lot carry on sales activities for it, including posting signs, and use a strata lot it owns or rents as a display lot for selling other lots in the plan [bc.spa.sched.bylaw30].

## When it applies to your building

Standard bylaw 30 is one of your building's bylaws unless, and to the extent that, a different bylaw on this subject is filed in the land title office [bc.spa.s120]. An owner developer may have filed different bylaws when the strata plan was deposited [bc.spa.s120], and owners may have amended them since by 3/4 vote and filing an Amendment to Bylaws; until that filing an amendment has no effect [bc.spa.s128]. So the question is always what the registered bylaws say, not what the standard bylaw says.

## What a building can change, and the limits

This bylaw matters while a development is selling and is mostly spent afterwards, so buildings often leave it in place unnoticed. The related power is the Act's: the corporation may pass a bylaw governing activities relating to the sale of a strata lot, including where signs go and when common property may be shown or open houses held, but that bylaw may not prohibit or unreasonably restrict those activities [bc.spa.s122]. That is the bylaw to look at for owner resales, not this one.

## What to check in the registered bylaws

Whether the building still has an owner developer with unsold lots; and whether there is a separate sale-activities bylaw, and if so whether its restrictions on signs and showings stay on the right side of [bc.spa.s122].
