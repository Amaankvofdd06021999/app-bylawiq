# Standard bylaw 24: fining a contravention that keeps going

## What it covers

Allows a fine every seven days where an activity or lack of activity that contravenes a bylaw or rule continues without interruption for longer than seven days [bc.spa.sched.bylaw24].

## When it applies to your building

Standard bylaw 24 is one of your building's bylaws unless, and to the extent that, a different bylaw on this subject is filed in the land title office [bc.spa.s120]. An owner developer may have filed different bylaws when the strata plan was deposited [bc.spa.s120], and owners may have amended them since by 3/4 vote and filing an Amendment to Bylaws; until that filing an amendment has no effect [bc.spa.s128]. So the question is always what the registered bylaws say, not what the standard bylaw says.

## What a building can change, and the limits

The Regulation caps how often a fine may be imposed for a continuing contravention at every seven days, except for a bylaw prohibiting or limiting short-term accommodation of a residential strata lot, where it may be daily [bc.spr.s7.1]; the bylaws may set the frequency within that cap [bc.spa.s132]. So a building that wants daily fines for short-term rentals has to amend this bylaw as well as standard bylaw 23 [bc.spa.sched.bylaw23] — raising the amount alone leaves the frequency at seven days. The procedural side is separate and does not repeat: once the corporation has complied with the complaint and answer requirements for a contravention, it may fine for a continuing contravention of that bylaw or rule without going through them again [bc.spa.s135].

## What to check in the registered bylaws

The frequency in the registered bylaws against the regulated cap; whether the "without interruption" wording survives, since a gap in the conduct restarts the analysis; and whether the first fine for the contravention followed the complaint and answer process, because the continuing-contravention shortcut only applies after that.
