# Standard bylaw 2: what an owner must repair and maintain

## What it covers

Puts the repair and maintenance of a strata lot on its owner, and the repair and maintenance of limited common property on the owner who has the use of it, in each case except for what the bylaws make the strata corporation's responsibility [bc.spa.sched.bylaw2]. It is the mirror of standard bylaw 8 and has to be read with it [bc.spa.sched.bylaw8].

## When it applies to your building

Standard bylaw 2 is one of your building's bylaws unless, and to the extent that, a different bylaw on this subject is filed in the land title office [bc.spa.s120]. An owner developer may have filed different bylaws when the strata plan was deposited [bc.spa.s120], and owners may have amended them since by 3/4 vote and filing an Amendment to Bylaws; until that filing an amendment has no effect [bc.spa.s128]. So the question is always what the registered bylaws say, not what the standard bylaw says.

## What a building can change, and the limits

This is the boundary most often moved, and the Act limits how far. A strata corporation may by bylaw make an owner responsible for limited common property the owner may use; it may make an owner responsible for other common property only if the Regulation identifies that property, and subject to the prescribed restrictions; and it may take on responsibility for specified portions of a strata lot [bc.spa.s72]. A bylaw of that last kind also changes who pays: where the corporation has taken responsibility for specified portions of some but not all strata lots, the cost is shared only among the lots it relates to, by a different formula [bc.spr.s6.5]. Windows, balconies, patio doors and in-lot plumbing are the usual subjects of an amendment.

## What to check in the registered bylaws

Which items the registered bylaws move in either direction, and whether a change that makes owners responsible for common property is one the Regulation actually permits. If responsibility for parts of some lots has moved to the corporation, check that the budget shares that cost the way the Regulation requires [bc.spr.s6.5].
