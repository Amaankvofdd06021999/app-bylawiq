# Standard bylaw 19: telling owners the council minutes

## What it covers

Requires council to inform owners of the minutes of every council meeting within two weeks of the meeting, whether or not the minutes have been approved [bc.spa.sched.bylaw19].

## When it applies to your building

Standard bylaw 19 is one of your building's bylaws unless, and to the extent that, a different bylaw on this subject is filed in the land title office [bc.spa.s120]. An owner developer may have filed different bylaws when the strata plan was deposited [bc.spa.s120], and owners may have amended them since by 3/4 vote and filing an Amendment to Bylaws; until that filing an amendment has no effect [bc.spa.s128]. So the question is always what the registered bylaws say, not what the standard bylaw says.

## What a building can change, and the limits

Amendments usually set out how owners are informed rather than changing the deadline. The deadline is independent of the records regime: the corporation must prepare minutes of council meetings including the results of votes [bc.spa.s35], and must make records available on request within the Act's time limits [bc.spa.s36]. Waiting for approval at the next meeting before circulating minutes does not comply with this bylaw.

## What to check in the registered bylaws

Whether the two weeks survives and how minutes are distributed; and whether the building is in fact circulating unapproved minutes, which is the common failure here.
