# Records requests

> **Not yet approved.** A BC-licensed lawyer has to review this before it is sent to anyone. The steps below cite the provision that requires them; the wording does not come from any approved precedent.

## Purpose

To answer requests for strata records inside the statutory deadlines, and to avoid both of the
failures: withholding a record the Act covers, and producing one it does not.

## Who may ask

An owner; a tenant who has been assigned the landlord's right to inspect and obtain copies; a person
authorised in writing by either; and a former owner or former tenant, for records relating to the
period when they were an owner or tenant, whenever those records were created [bc.spa.s36,
bc.spa.s147, bc.spa.s148]. A tenant may always obtain the bylaws and rules [bc.spa.s36].

**Do not ask why.** The Act does not make a reason a condition of access [bc.spa.s36].

## Deadlines

- **One week** where the request is for the bylaws or rules [bc.spa.s36].
- **Two weeks** for everything else [bc.spa.s36].
- An Information Certificate is **one week** from a request by an owner, purchaser or someone they
  authorise [bc.spa.s59].

Diary the deadline on the day the request arrives.

## The steps

1. **Record** the request: who asked, what was asked for, the date received, the deadline.
2. **Check standing**: owner, assigned tenant, authorised person, or former owner or tenant for
   their period [bc.spa.s36].
3. **Map each item to the Act's list.** The right of access runs to the records the corporation must
   prepare and retain [bc.spa.s35]. Something outside that list is not covered; something on it
   cannot be withheld because it is inconvenient.
4. **Check retention.** If a record no longer exists, check whether it should have: retention
   periods run from permanent, through six years and two years, down to current copies only
   [bc.spr.s4.1]. Say so plainly rather than leaving the item unanswered.
5. **Produce** by inspection or copies, as asked [bc.spa.s36].
6. **Escalate** to counsel before withholding anything, and before producing anything that names a
   person who is not the requester and is not part of a record the Act requires.
7. **File** the request, what was produced and the date.

## What this policy cannot settle

Whether privacy law permits or requires anything beyond the Strata Property Act. The
*Personal Information Protection Act* is not in the kb, so no position on it is taken here; that is
open question 29.

## Standing duty, separate from requests

Council must inform owners of the minutes of every council meeting within two weeks of the meeting,
whether or not they have been approved [bc.spa.sched.bylaw19]. That happens without anyone asking.
