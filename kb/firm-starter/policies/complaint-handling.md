# Complaint handling

> **Not yet approved.** A BC-licensed lawyer has to review this before it is sent to anyone. The steps below cite the provision that requires them; the wording does not come from any approved precedent.

## Purpose

One route for every bylaw or rule complaint, so that the statutory steps happen in the right order
and can be shown to have happened [bc.spa.s135].

## Scope

Every complaint about a contravention of a bylaw or rule, from any source, about an owner, tenant,
occupant or visitor. A complaint about a **council member** has its own provision and is not handled
under this policy [bc.spa.s136].

## Who decides what

The manager receives, records, acknowledges and prepares. **Council decides.** Whether a person
contravened, whether to fine and how much, and whether to deny a recreational facility cannot be
delegated [bc.spa.sched.bylaw20]. The corporation acts through council [bc.spa.s26].

## The steps

1. **Receive and record.** Date, source, what is alleged, when and where, and any evidence. The Act
   requires the corporation to have received a complaint before it penalises anyone
   [bc.spa.s135].
2. **Check the bylaw.** Confirm the bylaw or rule was registered and in force on the date of the
   incident, from the building's filed bylaws.
3. **Decide whether to proceed.** Council may warn, or give time to comply, instead of enforcing
   [bc.spa.s129].
4. **Give written particulars.** Send the section 135 notice to the owner or tenant, and, where the
   person is a tenant, notice of the complaint to the landlord and the owner [bc.spa.s135]. Use
   `firm.template.s135-notice` and `firm.template.tenant-contravention-notice`.
5. **Allow a reasonable opportunity to answer**, including a hearing if one is requested
   [bc.spa.s135]. A hearing means being heard in person at a council meeting [bc.spr.s7.2]; a
   request under [bc.spa.s34.1] must be heard within four weeks.
6. **Council decides**, at a meeting, and the vote goes in the minutes.
7. **Give written notice of the decision** as soon as feasible, to the person and to any landlord
   and owner who were notified [bc.spa.s135]. Use `firm.template.hearing-decision` or
   `firm.template.fine-notice`.
8. **File everything** together: complaint, notice, response, hearing record, decision, and the
   dates each was sent.

## Recording and privacy

The part of a council meeting dealing with a bylaw contravention hearing is closed to owners
attending as observers [bc.spa.sched.bylaw17]. Council must still inform owners of the minutes
within two weeks [bc.spa.sched.bylaw19], so minute the fact and outcome of a closed session without
reproducing the substance of the hearing in the copy sent to owners.

## Continuing contraventions

Once the process has been completed for a contravention of a bylaw or rule, a continuing
contravention of **that same** bylaw or rule may be penalised without repeating it [bc.spa.s135].
This does not extend to a different bylaw, and the frequency of fines is still limited by the
building's bylaws.

## What this policy does not do

It does not decide any particular complaint, and it does not replace advice on one. Where a
complaint involves a possible disability, a protected characteristic, harassment, or a threat to
safety, escalate under `firm.policy.accommodation-request` or to counsel before sending anything.
