# Firm starter kit

Templates, policies, guidance and a legal tracker a property management firm can adopt on day one. Layer `firm`, licence `bylawiq-original`.

Everything here gives advice or is sent to owners, so nothing is `approved` without counsel review. Until then, set `notes: needs counsel review`.

- `templates/` (type `template`): s.135 notice, hearing response, decision letter, fine notice.
- `policies/` (type `policy`): complaint handling SOP.
- `guidance/` (type `guidance`): how-to notes for managers.
- `legal-tracker/` (type `tracker`): recent law changes and decisions firms should know about.
