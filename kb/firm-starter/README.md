# Firm starter kit

Templates, policies, guidance and a legal tracker a property management firm can adopt on day one. Layer `firm`, licence `bylawiq-original`.

Everything here gives advice or is sent to owners, so nothing is `approved` without counsel review. Until then, set `notes: needs counsel review`.

- `templates/` (type `template`): the five letters a bylaw enforcement file needs — the section 135
  notice of complaint, the notice to a landlord and owner about a tenant, the response to a hearing
  request, the decision letter after a hearing, and the notice of decision imposing a fine.
  Placeholders are `{{double_braces}}`, and each step cites the provision that requires it.
- `policies/` (type `policy`): complaint handling, records requests, accommodation requests.
- `guidance/` (type `guidance`): running a section 135 process start to finish, handling an
  accommodation request, responding to a records request.
- `legal-tracker/` (type `tracker`): one item per change a firm needs to know about.

Two of these are deliberately thin, and say so in the item. The accommodation policy and guidance
give a **process only**: the *Human Rights Code* is not in the kb (task A5), so they do not state
when accommodation is required or what undue hardship means. The legal tracker has a single entry,
because filling it needs the Tables of Legislative Changes (task A2) and decision summaries
(phase C3), both of which are blocked.
