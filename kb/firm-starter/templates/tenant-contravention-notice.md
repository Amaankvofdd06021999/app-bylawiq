# Notice to a landlord and owner about a tenant's contravention

> **Not yet approved.** A BC-licensed lawyer has to review this before it is sent to anyone. The steps below cite the provision that requires them; the wording does not come from any approved precedent.

## When to use this

Whenever the person complained about is a **tenant**. The Act makes notice of the complaint to the
tenant's landlord and to the owner a condition of imposing a fine, requiring payment of the costs of
remedying a contravention, or denying a recreational facility [bc.spa.s135]. Send it at the same
time as the notice to the tenant, not afterwards.

## What the Act requires

- **Notice of the complaint** to the landlord and the owner, before any penalty [bc.spa.s135].
- The landlord and owner are **answerable for the tenant's fines** and the costs of remedying a
  contravention; the corporation may collect from the tenant, the landlord and the owner, but not
  more than the total once over, and a landlord or owner who pays is owed that amount by the tenant
  [bc.spa.s131].
- A repeated or continuing contravention of a reasonable and significant bylaw or rule is an event
  allowing the **landlord** to end the tenancy under the *Residential Tenancy Act*
  [bc.spa.s137], and, where it also seriously interferes with another person's use and enjoyment,
  allowing the **strata corporation** to give that notice [bc.spa.s138]. The
  *Residential Tenancy Act* is not in the kb, so its requirements are not stated here.

## Before you send it

1. Identify the landlord and owner from the corporation's records [bc.spa.s147], and check whether
   the landlord has filed the signed Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities [bc.spa.s146].
2. Send this **with** the notice to the tenant, so the timing cannot be questioned
   [bc.spa.s135].
3. Do not include anything about the tenant beyond the particulars of the complaint.

## Template

> {{strata_plan_number}} · {{building_name}}
> {{date_sent}}
>
> To: {{landlord_name}} and {{owner_name}}, {{unit_or_strata_lot}}
>
> **Re: Complaint about the tenant of {{unit_or_strata_lot}}**
>
> The strata corporation has received a complaint that on {{incident_dates}} the tenant of
> {{unit_or_strata_lot}} {{incident_particulars}}, said to contravene bylaw
> {{bylaw_number}}.
>
> A notice setting out the particulars of the complaint has been sent to the tenant today, and the
> tenant has been given an opportunity to answer it, including by requesting a hearing before
> council. This letter gives you notice of the complaint, as the Strata Property Act requires before
> the strata corporation imposes any fine or other penalty.
>
> No decision has been made. If council decides that a contravention occurred and imposes a fine or
> requires payment of the costs of remedying it, the strata corporation may collect that amount from
> the tenant, from you as landlord, or from the owner, though not more than the amount of the fine or
> costs in total. If you pay, the tenant owes you what you paid.
>
> You may also send council anything you want it to consider, by {{response_deadline}}.
>
> {{sender_name}}, on behalf of the strata council of {{strata_plan_number}}
