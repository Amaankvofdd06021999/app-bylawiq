# Response to a request for a council hearing

> **Not yet approved.** A BC-licensed lawyer has to review this before it is sent to anyone. The steps below cite the provision that requires them; the wording does not come from any approved precedent.

## When to use this

When an owner or tenant has asked in writing for a hearing at a council meeting, whether as part of
answering a bylaw complaint [bc.spa.s135] or on their own initiative [bc.spa.s34.1]. This letter
confirms the hearing and its arrangements. It is not the decision.

## What the Act requires

- An owner or tenant may request a hearing **by application in writing stating the reason**, and
  council **must hold** a council meeting to hear them **within four weeks** after the request
  [bc.spa.s34.1].
- A hearing means an **opportunity to be heard in person at a council meeting** [bc.spr.s7.2].
- If the purpose of the hearing is to seek a decision of council, council must give a **written
  decision within one week** after the hearing [bc.spa.s34.1].
- Under the standard bylaws, the part of a council meeting dealing with a bylaw contravention
  hearing is **closed to owners attending as observers** [bc.spa.sched.bylaw17].

## Before you send it

1. Diary the **four-week deadline** from the date the request was received [bc.spa.s34.1], and
   check council can meet inside it; any council member may call a meeting on a week's notice, or
   less with consent [bc.spa.sched.bylaw14].
2. Work out whether the purpose is **to seek a decision**, because that sets the one-week written
   decision duty [bc.spa.s34.1].
3. Do not treat a request as invalid for not using a form. The Act asks for a written application
   stating the reason [bc.spa.s34.1].

>
> {{decision_sentence}}
>
> If {{hearing_date}} does not work for you, contact {{contact}} as soon as possible so that
> another date inside the four week period can be arranged.
>
> {{sender_name}}, on behalf of the strata council of {{strata_plan_number}}

## Fields

| Placeholder | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `{{request_received_date}}` | The date the written request arrived; the four weeks run from it [bc.spa.s34.1] |
| `{{hearing_date}}` | Must be within four weeks of the request [bc.spa.s34.1] |
| `{{hearing_location_or_link}}` | Where council meets. A hearing means being heard in person at a council meeting [bc.spr.s7.2]; whether a video meeting satisfies that is not settled, so check with counsel before offering one |
| `{{decision_sentence}}` | Where the purpose is to seek a decision: "Council will give you its decision in writing within one week after the hearing." [bc.spa.s34.1] Otherwise omit |
