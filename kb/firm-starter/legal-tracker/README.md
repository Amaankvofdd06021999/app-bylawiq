# legal-tracker

One item per change a firm needs to act on: what changed, when, who is affected and what to do. Currently one entry, on the 2022 removal of rental restrictions. Filling it out needs the Tables of Legislative Changes (task A2) and decision summaries (phase C3).

Everything here gives advice or is sent to owners, so nothing is `approved` without counsel review; until then each item's `notes` says `needs counsel review`. See `../README.md`.
