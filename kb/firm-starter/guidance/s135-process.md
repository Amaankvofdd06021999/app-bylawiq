# Running a section 135 process from start to finish

> **Not yet approved.** A BC-licensed lawyer has to review this before it is sent to anyone. The steps below cite the provision that requires them; the wording does not come from any approved precedent.

A walk-through for a manager, from the complaint arriving to the fine being recorded. The policy is
`firm.policy.complaint-handling`; this is how it goes in practice, and where it usually comes apart.

## Stage 1 — The complaint arrives

Record it the day it arrives. The Act requires the corporation to have **received a complaint**
before it fines anyone, requires payment of the costs of remedying a contravention, or denies a
recreational facility [bc.spa.s135]. A council member's own observation, with nothing else, is worth
checking against that requirement before going further.

Identify the bylaw from the **building's registered bylaws**. A bylaw that was passed but never
filed is not one of the building's bylaws.

## Stage 2 — Decide whether to enforce at all

Council may warn, or give time to comply [bc.spa.s129]. That is often the proportionate step, and it
costs nothing procedurally. The three enforcement options are a fine, remedying the contravention,
and denying a recreational facility [bc.spa.s130, bc.spa.s133, bc.spa.s134].

## Stage 3 — Give the particulars

Send the written particulars of the complaint, and say how and by when the person may answer, and
that they may request a hearing [bc.spa.s135]. Use `firm.template.s135-notice`.

**Where the person is a tenant**, send notice of the complaint to the landlord and the owner at the
same time [bc.spa.s135], with `firm.template.tenant-contravention-notice`. They are answerable for
the fine [bc.spa.s131].

*Where this comes apart:* a letter that says a fine has been imposed and invites a reply. That is the
decision before the opportunity to answer, and the order is what the section is about.

## Stage 4 — The opportunity to answer

Give a reasonable period, and mean it. If a hearing is requested, hold one: a hearing is an
**opportunity to be heard in person at a council meeting** [bc.spr.s7.2], not an exchange of
letters. A request under [bc.spa.s34.1] must be heard within four weeks.

*Where this comes apart:* offering "the chance to put it in writing" in answer to a request for a
hearing.

## Stage 5 — Council decides

At a meeting, by council. The decisions whether a person contravened, whether to fine and how much,
and whether to deny a recreational facility **cannot be delegated** to the manager
[bc.spa.sched.bylaw20]. Record the vote in the minutes [bc.spa.sched.bylaw18].

Check the amount against the **building's own maximum**, not the Regulation's. The Regulation caps
the bylaws; the bylaws cap the fine [bc.spa.s132, bc.spr.s7.1]. Under unamended standard bylaws the
maximum is $50 for a bylaw and $10 for a rule [bc.spa.sched.bylaw23].

*Where this comes apart:* the manager issues the fine and council ratifies it later.

## Stage 6 — Give the decision in writing

As soon as feasible, to the person and to any landlord and owner who were notified
[bc.spa.s135]. Use `firm.template.fine-notice` or `firm.template.hearing-decision`.

*Where this comes apart:* the fine appears on the account and no decision letter is ever sent.

## Stage 7 — If it continues

Once the corporation has complied with the section for a contravention, it may fine for a
**continuing contravention of that same bylaw or rule** without repeating the process
[bc.spa.s135] — subject to the frequency in the building's bylaws, within the regulated limit of
every 7 days, or daily for a short-term accommodation bylaw [bc.spa.sched.bylaw24, bc.spr.s7.1].

*Where this comes apart:* using the shortcut for a different bylaw, or for a fresh contravention
after the conduct had stopped.

## The file at the end

Complaint, written particulars and the date sent, notices to landlord and owner, the response,
the hearing request and what was arranged, the minutes showing council's decision and the vote, the
written decision and the date sent, and the ledger entry. If the fine is challenged, this file is
the answer.
