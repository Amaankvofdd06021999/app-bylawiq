# Building document acquisition checklist

Research draft. Inclusion does not assert that every document is legally mandatory, available to every resident, or necessary for every building. Exact statutory locators and exceptions must be verified before promotion. Access labels below are product design suggestions, not determinations of legal entitlement.

| ID | Document | Why it matters | Acquisition and verification | Suggested access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Registered strata plan and amendments | Boundary, lot/common/LCP classification; building/section identity | Registry copy; keep diagram page references | building-restricted |
| D02 | Complete filed bylaws and amendment chain | Determine operative clause on the relevant date | Obtain full index and each operative filing; reconcile repeals | building-restricted |
| D03 | Current rules and ratification history | Identify rules still in effect and their scope | Signed/current rules plus adopting and ratifying minutes | building-restricted |
| D04 | AGM/SGM notices, resolutions and minutes | Authority for budgets, levies, bylaw changes and votes | Match final resolutions to vote records and later filings | building-restricted |
| D05 | Council bylaws, membership, minutes and delegations | Decision authority, conflicts and process | Restrict confidential attachments; distinguish draft/final minutes | role-restricted |
| D06 | Current budget and financial statements | Fee allocation and operating/reserve context | Record fiscal period and approval resolution | building-restricted |
| D07 | Unit-entitlement and allocation schedules | Support allocation rather than assume equal shares | Use registered schedule and any valid alternative basis | building-restricted |
| D08 | Special-levy resolutions and payment schedules | Identify purpose, authorization, amounts and due dates | Separate resolution from individual ledgers | building-restricted |
| D09 | Account and arrears ledger | Distinguish fees, levies, interest, fines and chargebacks | Reconcile opening balance; access limited to proper role | highly-restricted |
| D10 | Depreciation report and updates | Report compliance, asset inventory and long-term funding | Record author qualification, report date and supersession | building-restricted |
| D11 | Electrical planning report and updates | Deadline classification and owner-request trigger | Do not substitute an EV-ready plan for the EPR | building-restricted |
| D12 | Insurance policy, endorsements, summary and appraisal | Identify actual coverage, limits, exclusions and deductibles | Policy term and endorsements outrank a stale summary for factual extraction | role-restricted |
| D13 | Form B and all attachments | Transaction-date disclosure and document completeness | Record request and issue dates; check section certificate needs | authorized-recipient |
| D14 | Form F and supporting account record | Transfer-related certificate workflow | Exact issuance rules and statutory source still to verify | authorized-recipient |
| D15 | Form K and assignments of owner powers | Tenant status and rights without assuming family assignment | Minimize personal data; record effective period | highly-restricted |
| D16 | Parking/storage designations and agreements | Differentiate property rights from revocable permission | Tie plan reference to lease, licence or permission instrument | building-restricted |
| D17 | Alteration approvals, agreements and drawings | Approval conditions and ongoing maintenance allocation | Keep signed version, affected component and applicable bylaw | role-restricted |
| D18 | Repair contracts, engineering reports and maintenance log | Separate physical facts from disputed legal responsibility | Date reports and scope; retain source diagrams as authorized | role-restricted |
| D19 | Complaint, response, hearing and decision records | Reconstruct process and evidence without assuming liability | Keep separate restricted case workspace; service/delivery proof | highly-restricted |
| D20 | Accommodation request and decision file | Track requests and alternatives without overexposing health information | Separate functional needs from unnecessary medical details | highly-restricted |
| D21 | Management agreement, disclosures and authority | Identify the firm’s actual mandate and conflicts | Do not infer power from a generic firm template | firm-and-council-restricted |
| D22 | Privacy policy, retention schedule and vendor agreements | Map purposes, access, safeguards and retention decisions | Policy content requires current PIPA/OIPC review | role-restricted |
| D23 | Section/type and shared-facility agreements | Identify responsible entity and expense boundaries | Keep entity scope distinct in retrieval metadata | role-restricted |
| D24 | Developer turnover, warranty and first-AGM package | Creation-stage rules and specialist follow-up | Primary source checklist still required for exact duties | building-restricted |
| D25 | Local permits, licences and inspection records | Support municipality-specific and safety questions | Store jurisdiction and validity period; rights review before reuse | role-restricted |

Authority and provenance checklist: authorized supplier; actual building and section scope; source date and document version; complete pages and attachments; OCR quality; signature/filing evidence where relevant; confidentiality; retention and deletion basis; duplicate/superseded status. No building documents or private data were acquired during this research.
