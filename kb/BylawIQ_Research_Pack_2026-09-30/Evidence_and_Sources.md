# Evidence notes and source register

Draft research, retrieved 2026-09-30. These are concise original research notes. They are not imported statute items, approved legal advice, or decision holdings. Required production citation IDs remain unresolved until the actual repository is available.

## Evidence notes

### F01 — bylaw-enforcement-s135

Before imposing the sanctions addressed by SPA s.135, the strata must receive a complaint, give written particulars and a reasonable opportunity to respond, including a hearing if requested, and give the required written decision. Tenant allegations also require notice to the landlord and owner.

Source: [Strata Property Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_00_multi). Locator: s.135. Promotion gate: primary_passage_read; map_existing_item_and_review.

### F02 — fines

The provincial guidance distinguishes maximum bylaw fines of $200, rule fines of $50, and qualifying short-term-rental bylaw fines of $1,000. A building’s valid bylaw may set a lower amount. Continuing contraventions and short-term rentals have different permitted frequencies.

Source: [Enforcing strata bylaws and rules - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/enforcing-bylaws-and-rules). Locator: Fining; maximum fines; frequency. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F03 — fines

The Standard Bylaws’ fine amounts differ from the regulatory ceilings. Do not substitute a maximum for the fine actually authorized by the applicable building bylaw.

Source: [Enforcing strata bylaws and rules - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/enforcing-bylaws-and-rules). Locator: Fining; Standard Bylaws. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F04 — hearings

An owner or tenant may request a council hearing in writing with reasons. The hearing must be held within four weeks; if a decision is requested, the written decision is due within one week after the hearing.

Source: [Strata council meetings - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/meetings-and-voting/council-meetings). Locator: Requesting a council hearing; SPA s.34.1 as identified by guidance. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F05 — bylaws-rules

Rules concern common property and common assets; they cannot regulate a strata lot’s use. Bylaws have broader scope, and a bylaw prevails over a conflicting rule. New rules require ratification at the next general meeting to continue.

Source: [Strata bylaws and rules explained - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/bylaws-and-rules-explained). Locator: Rules; bylaws; ratification. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F06 — bylaw-amendments

Bylaw amendment voting can differ by development stage and residential/non-residential mix. Filing is a separate step. Registration does not itself establish enforceability, and a registry index may contain obsolete amendments.

Source: [Amending strata bylaws and rules - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/amending-bylaws-and-rules). Locator: Voting; filing; getting copies. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F07 — pets

The default pet bylaw permits one dog or one cat, alongside specified other pet categories. Filed bylaws may differ. The pet grandfathering reference date is when the restrictive bylaw is passed; a replacement pet is not automatically protected.

Source: [Strata pet bylaws - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/pet-bylaws). Locator: Standard pet bylaw; grandfathering. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F08 — pets

Certified guide/service dogs have statutory protection; an uncertified animal may still raise a Human Rights Code accommodation issue. Certification alone is not the complete accommodation test.

Source: [Strata pet bylaws - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/pet-bylaws). Locator: Guide dogs, service dogs and human rights. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F09 — smoking-cannabis

Smoking restrictions may be enacted through bylaws covering lots and shared areas, while rules have narrower scope. Nuisance provisions can matter without an express smoking ban. Medical-use accommodation and other legislation require separate analysis.

Source: [Strata non-smoking bylaws - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/non-smoking-bylaws). Locator: Scope; nuisance; medical cannabis. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F10 — age-restrictions

Since November 24, 2022, strata age-restriction bylaws may use 55 or a higher age. Ownership and residency are distinct. Existing residents, caregivers, certain children and spouses can engage exemptions; expanded exemptions took effect May 1, 2023.

Source: [Strata age-restriction bylaws - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws). Locator: Permitted age restrictions; exemptions. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F11 — rentals

Strata rental-restriction bylaws ceased to be permitted on November 24, 2022. That change did not remove strata authority to restrict short-term rentals.

Source: [Strata short-term rental bylaws - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/short-term-rental-bylaws). Locator: Distinction from longer-term rentals. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F12 — short-term-rentals

Provincial, local-government and strata short-term-rental controls must be considered separately. The provincial guidance says platform information supplied to the Province is not shared with strata corporations.

Source: [Strata short-term rental bylaws - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/short-term-rental-bylaws). Locator: Provincial legislation; data sharing. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F13 — tenant-rights-form-k

SPA s.146 requires landlord delivery of bylaws, rules and Form K before renting, and delivery of the signed acknowledgment to the strata within two weeks. Failure to provide Form K does not exempt tenants from bylaws.

Source: [Strata Property Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_00_multi). Locator: s.146. Promotion gate: primary_passage_read; map_existing_item_and_review.

### F14 — tenant-rights-form-k

The current tenant guidance says the former automatic assignment for family-member tenants ended November 24, 2022. Rights now require the applicable assignment analysis; a three-year-or-longer fixed tenancy has a distinct statutory regime.

Source: [Tenants in stratas - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/renting-in-stratas/tenants-in-stratas). Locator: Assignment of landlord powers and duties. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F15 — privacy-records

SPA s.36 has requester-specific rights: owners, qualifying assigned tenants, authorized people, and certain former owners/tenants. All tenants have access to bylaws and rules. Response periods differ: one week for bylaws/rules, two weeks for other covered records.

Source: [Strata Property Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_00_multi). Locator: s.36. Promotion gate: primary_passage_read; map_existing_item_and_review.

### F16 — privacy-records

The Province describes free inspection, a copying ceiling of 25 cents per page, and record-category-specific retention. Its records page also identifies PIPA as applicable to strata corporations.

Source: [Information and record keeping in stratas - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/information-and-record-keeping). Locator: Access; fees; retention; privacy. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F17 — human-rights-accommodation

The Tribunal’s strata guidance describes discrimination as adverse impact connected to a protected characteristic; intent is not required. Accommodation requires reasonable and practical steps, with undue hardship assessed on evidence.

Source: [Strata - BC Human Rights Tribunal](https://www.bchrt.bc.ca/human-rights-duties/housing/strata/). Locator: What is discrimination; accommodation; justification. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F18 — human-rights-accommodation

The Tribunal identifies strata services to owners within the Human Rights Code’s services framework under s.8. Research limited to tenancy discrimination would therefore leave an important gap.

Source: [Leading cases: Services customarily available to the public - BC Human Rights Tribunal](https://www.bchrt.bc.ca/law-library/leading-cases/services-public/). Locator: Section 8; strata services example. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F19 — repairs-maintenance

Repair responsibility depends on the property category and applicable bylaws. The Province distinguishes strata responsibility for common property/assets from lot-owner responsibilities and possible bylaw variations.

Source: [Division of repair duties in a strata - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/division-of-repair-duties). Locator: Division of repair and maintenance duties. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F20 — insurance-deductibles

Strata insurance, an owner’s insurance, repair obligations and ultimate deductible recovery are separate questions. The Province identifies insurance duties for strata corporations regardless of strata type or size; actual policies and bylaws remain necessary.

Source: [Insurance for strata corporations - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances-and-insurance/strata-corporation-insurance). Locator: Required insurance; deductible; owner insurance. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F21 — depreciation-reports

Stratas with at least five lots require depreciation reports on a five-year cycle. Annual waiver votes ended July 1, 2024. Transition deadlines depend on location and the date of an existing report.

Source: [Strata depreciation report requirements - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements). Locator: Updated requirements; existing strata corporations. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F22 — depreciation-reports

For applicable existing stratas without a report, or with one before December 31, 2020, the deadline was July 1, 2026 in Metro Vancouver, Fraser Valley and Capital regional districts, with the air/boat-only island exception. The corresponding remaining-BC deadline is July 1, 2027.

Source: [Strata depreciation report requirements - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements). Locator: Transition deadlines. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F23 — electrical-planning

For applicable existing stratas with at least five lots, electrical planning reports are due December 31, 2026 in the three specified regional districts, subject to the island exception, or December 31, 2028 elsewhere. New strata corporations have separate timing.

Source: [Strata electrical planning report - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/the-environment/electrical-planning-report). Locator: Who needs a report; deadlines. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F24 — ev-charging

Obtaining an electrical planning report, or passing the applicable deadline, activates the owner-request framework described by the Province. That framework includes a three-month decision period. An electrical planning report is distinct from an EV-ready plan.

Source: [Strata electrical planning report - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/the-environment/electrical-planning-report). Locator: EV charging requests; EPR compared with EV-ready plan. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F25 — form-b-disclosure

Form B must use the current version and be supplied within seven days of a proper request. Required attachments include rules, the current budget, the latest depreciation report and electrical planning reports. Insurance information and parking/storage rights are also addressed.

Source: [Form B: Information Certificate - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). Locator: Deadline; information; attachments. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F26 — form-b-disclosure

The Province describes a maximum basic Form B charge of $35, plus up to 25 cents per copied page. Sections may require separate certificates.

Source: [Form B: Information Certificate - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). Locator: Fees; sections. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F27 — parking-storage

Parking/storage rights may arise from the lot, limited common property, common-property permission, or a lease/licence. Ordinary exclusive-use permissions and qualifying EV parking permissions have different duration rules; the plan and actual agreements matter.

Source: [Strata parking stalls and storage lockers - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/parking-and-storage). Locator: Ownership; LCP; common property; EV stalls. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F28 — contingency-reserve-fund

The minimum annual CRF contribution became 10% of the budgeted operating-fund contribution from November 1, 2023. This is a contribution floor, not a finding that the reserve balance or long-term funding is adequate.

Source: [The contingency reserve fund (CRF) in strata corporations - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances-and-insurance/the-contingency-reserve-fund-crf). Locator: Minimum contribution; planning; spending. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F29 — strata-fees-arrears

Budgets, fee allocation, late-payment interest, fines and lienable arrears require separate treatment. The Province identifies majority budget approval and unit-entitlement allocation, subject to applicable statutory exceptions.

Source: [Budgeting and strata fees - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances-and-insurance/budgeting-and-strata-fees). Locator: Budget approval; allocation; collection. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F30 — special-levies

A special levy resolution must specify purpose, total amount, allocation and payment timing. The Province distinguishes the usual three-quarter vote from unanimous approval for a different allocation method.

Source: [Special levies in stratas - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances-and-insurance/special-levies). Locator: Approval; resolution; allocation. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F31 — voting-proxies

A majority vote and a three-quarter vote generally use votes cast by eligible voters present personally or by proxy, excluding abstentions. An 80% or unanimous vote uses all eligible strata votes. The specific decision can change the required threshold.

Source: [Types of strata voting - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/meetings-and-voting/types-of-voting). Locator: Voting thresholds; abstentions; exceptions. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F32 — crt-process

The CRT describes jurisdiction over most strata disputes of any monetary amount, with exclusions such as certain land/title and forced-sale matters. The ordinary small-claims cap must not be applied mechanically to strata claims.

Source: [Strata Property - BC Civil Resolution Tribunal](https://civilresolutionbc.ca/solution-explorer/strata/). Locator: What the CRT can and cannot decide. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F33 — crt-process

The current CRT rules index identifies Standard Rules effective February 1, 2026 and says procedural versions can depend on when a claim was filed, processed or decided.

Source: [Rules and Policies - BC Civil Resolution Tribunal](https://civilresolutionbc.ca/resources/rules-and-policies/). Locator: Current rules; older versions. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F34 — crt-process

The CRT fee page lists strata applications at $125 online or $150 by email/mail, and a $100 final-decision fee. Individual fee-waiver eligibility does not extend to strata corporations. These amounts require ongoing refresh.

Source: [Fees - BC Civil Resolution Tribunal](https://civilresolutionbc.ca/resources/fees/). Locator: Strata fees; fee waivers. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F35 — tenancy-disputes

RTB Policy Guideline 27 describes a limited SPA s.138 route into the RTA s.47 tenancy process for repeated/continuing serious bylaw contraventions. The RTB addresses the tenancy notice; the CRT addresses strata bylaw validity. Its vacation-versus-tenancy classification depends on the agreement and facts.

Source: [Policy Guideline 27: Jurisdiction ](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/policy-guidelines/gl27.pdf). Locator: Section K, PDF pages 14–15; section I, pages 11–12. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F36 — tenancy-disputes

The same RTB guidance says the CRT cannot cancel an RTB possession order or change an RTB tenancy-ending decision; judicial review is a separate Supreme Court route.

Source: [Policy Guideline 27: Jurisdiction ](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/policy-guidelines/gl27.pdf). Locator: Section K, PDF page 15. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F37 — management-agents

BCFSA describes a written management service agreement, remuneration/benefit disclosures and conflict management. It distinguishes professional regulation under real-estate-services law from enforcing the SPA or deciding contractual disputes.

Source: [Working with a Strata Management Company | BCFSA](https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-professional-resources/knowledge-base/strata-management-resources/working-with-strata-management-company). Locator: Service agreement; disclosures; conflicts; complaints. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F38 — building-onboarding

LTSA identifies the land-title registry as a source for strata plans and filed documents. Operational records and certificates generally require the strata, its manager, or an authorized document service; they are not all available from public web research.

Source: [Find Strata Property Information - LTSA](https://ltsa.ca/property-owners/how-can-i/find-strata-property-information/). Locator: Sources of strata records. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F39 — building-onboarding

The strata-plan general index can list amendments and other registered documents. Searching that index is chargeable even when no entries are found; a free common-property search is a different product.

Source: [Strata Plan General Index | System Help](https://help.ltsa.ca/ltsa-enterprise/strata-plan-general-index). Locator: General index; fees. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F40 — legal-changes

The provincial update page records removal in November 2025 of 28 former strata guides last updated in 2013. Those historical guides should not be treated as current guidance.

Source: [Subscribing for updates - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates). Locator: November 2025 update. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F41 — human-rights-process

The BCHRT changed dismissal-application procedure effective May 1, 2026. Its transition direction differentiates complaints by whether disclosure obligations had already been notified. A single timeless procedure would lose that distinction.

Source: [Transition to amended Rules of Practice and Procedure May 1, 2026 - BC Human Rights Tribunal](https://www.bchrt.bc.ca/law-library/practice-directions/transition-to-amended-rules-of-practice-and-procedure-may-1-2026/). Locator: Application of amendment; transition. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.

### F42 — legislation-scope

The Province’s legislation index identifies the Strata Property Act and three strata regulations, including the Bare Land Strata Regulations and Bare Land Strata Plan Cancellation Regulation. A corpus containing only the main Strata Property Regulation is not the full strata legislation set.

Source: [Strata legislation - Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/strata-legislation). Locator: Strata legislation; other relevant legislation. Promotion gate: verify_primary_provision_and_resolve_kb_id.
