# Who repairs and maintains what

## Summary

The corporation must repair and maintain common property and common assets; a bylaw can shift limited common property the owner uses onto that owner, can shift other common property only where the Regulation identifies it, and can move specified portions of a strata lot onto the corporation [bc.spa.s72]. The standard bylaws divide it in four tiers [bc.spa.sched.bylaw8]. What a thing *is* on the strata plan decides the rest.

## What the law says

**The statutory division.** Subject to what follows, the corporation must repair and maintain common property and common assets. By bylaw it may make an owner responsible for limited common property the owner has a right to use, or for other common property only if identified in the regulations and subject to prescribed restrictions. By bylaw it may take responsibility for specified portions of a strata lot [bc.spa.s72]. Where it has taken on portions of some lots but not all, the contribution or levy for that work is shared only among the lots it relates to, by a different formula [bc.spr.s6.5].

**Work orders.** Where the corporation receives a notice or order from a public or local authority requiring work on a strata lot, it must promptly give it to the owner, who must do the work; if the owner does not, the corporation may do it, must wait for an appeal if told of one in writing, and except in an emergency must give the owner a week's written notice of its intention [bc.spa.s84, bc.spa.s85].

**Remedying a contravention.** The corporation may do what is reasonably necessary to remedy a contravention of its bylaws or rules, including doing work on a strata lot or common property and removing objects, and may require the reasonable costs from the person who could be fined [bc.spa.s133].

## What the standard bylaws say

Standard bylaw 2 puts the strata lot and the limited common property the owner uses on the owner, except what the bylaws make the corporation's [bc.spa.sched.bylaw2]. Standard bylaw 8 gives the corporation common assets; common property that is not limited common property; limited common property for work occurring less often than once a year plus a listed set of building elements; and, in a plan that is not bare land, that same listed set within a strata lot [bc.spa.sched.bylaw8]. Standard bylaw 7 governs entry to do the work [bc.spa.sched.bylaw7]. See [building.standard-bylaw.8].

## Common mistakes

- Reading either standard bylaw 2 or 8 on its own. They are one division and have to be read together [bc.spa.sched.bylaw2, bc.spa.sched.bylaw8].
- Shifting common property repair onto owners where the Regulation does not permit it [bc.spa.s72].
- Amending one of the two bylaws and not the other, leaving an item with nobody responsible.
- Doing work under a work order without the week's notice, outside an emergency [bc.spa.s85].
- Treating the power to remedy a contravention as a general power to do work and charge for it [bc.spa.s133].
