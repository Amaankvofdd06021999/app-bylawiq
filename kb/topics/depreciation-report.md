# Depreciation reports

## Summary

A depreciation report is required from a qualified person, at least every 5 years, unless the strata plan has fewer than 5 strata lots [bc.spr.s6.21, bc.spr.s6.22, bc.spa.s94]. It matters beyond compliance: the reserve fund contribution must be set after considering the most recent report [bc.spr.s6.1], and whether reserve spending needs a majority or a 3/4 vote can turn on whether the work is recommended in it [bc.spa.s96].

## What the law says

**The requirement.** Subject to the regulations, a strata corporation must obtain from a qualified person, by the dates the regulations set, a depreciation report estimating the repair and replacement cost for major items and their expected life, containing the information the regulations require [bc.spa.s94].

**Who is qualified.** A person with the knowledge and expertise to understand the components, scope and complexity of the property the corporation must maintain or repair and, for a report obtained on or after 1 July 2025, a person in one of the listed professional categories [bc.spr.s6.2].

**What it must contain.** A physical component inventory and evaluation based on an on-site visual inspection, with a description and estimated service life over 30 years of the listed items including structure, exterior, systems and amenities; a summary of repairs and maintenance for those items; a financial forecasting section; the provider's name, qualifications, error and omission insurance and relationship to the corporation; the date; and an executive summary [bc.spr.s6.2].

**When.** At least once every 5 years. A corporation established before 1 July 2024 that had not obtained one since 31 December 2020 had to obtain one before 1 July 2026 if wholly or partly in the Capital Regional District, the Fraser Valley Regional District or the Metro Vancouver Regional District (excluding islands reachable only by air or boat), and otherwise before 1 July 2027. A corporation established on or after 1 July 2024 and before 1 July 2027 must obtain its first no later than 2 years after its first annual general meeting; one established on or after 1 July 2027, no later than 18 months after [bc.spr.s6.21]. The requirement does not apply while the strata plan has fewer than 5 strata lots [bc.spr.s6.22].

**What it drives.** The reserve fund contribution must be determined after considering the most recent report [bc.spr.s6.1]. Reserve fund spending related to repair, maintenance or replacement recommended in the most current report can be approved by majority vote rather than 3/4 [bc.spa.s96]. Reports must be retained permanently and produced on a records request [bc.spa.s35, bc.spa.s36, bc.spr.s4.1].

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the requirement can still be waived by a 3/4 vote each year.
- Missing that the 5 year cycle runs from the last report, not from a fixed date [bc.spr.s6.21].
- Commissioning a report from someone outside the qualified categories for a report obtained on or after 1 July 2025 [bc.spr.s6.2].
- Setting the reserve contribution without considering the most recent report [bc.spr.s6.1].
- Using a 3/4 vote where the work is recommended in the most current report and a majority would do [bc.spa.s96].
- Withholding a draft report on a records request, when the retention duty is for any report obtained [bc.spa.s35].
