# Licensing register

Every kb item's `licence` field must be an id from the table below. `tools/validate.ts` reads the ids from the first column (the value in backticks), so keep one licence per row and keep the id in backticks.

The terms below come from the licensing research of 2026-09-30. The full report, with verbatim quotes of every licence and a fetch log, is in `licensing-research.md` next to this file. It is research, not legal advice: the counsel questions it raised are in `open-questions.md`, and nothing here is final until counsel signs off.

**Status** means: **Verified** (terms read on the primary source), **Verified: permission required** (terms read, and they require written permission we do not have yet), **Unverified** (no terms found; treat as all rights reserved). Only `bc-kings-printer` and `bylawiq-original` allow verbatim text today. Every other licence means a structured summary in our own words plus a link to the source.

## Licences considered and not used

- **Open Government Licence – British Columbia (OGL-BC), version 2.0.** It applies only to records in the B.C. Data Catalogue that specify it. It does not cover BC statutes and regulations (King's Printer Licence), court decisions (the courts' own policy) or CRT decisions (no licence found). The BC Laws API has a Data Catalogue entry, but the API page states that the King's Printer Licence governs the content. It is deliberately not a row above, so the validator rejects it as a licence id. Source: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/data/policy-standards/open-data/open-government-licence-bc (archived 2025-04-09). High confidence that it does not apply.
- **Open Government Licence – Canada.** Federal data only; irrelevant to BC law.
