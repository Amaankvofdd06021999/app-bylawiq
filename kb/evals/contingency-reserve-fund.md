# Evals for the contingency reserve fund

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: Minimum contribution

- **Question:** Is there a minimum we must put into the reserve fund?
- **Scope:** any building past the year following its first annual general meeting.
- **Expected points:** Yes. The annual contribution must be at least 10% of the total amount budgeted for the operating fund contribution for the current fiscal year, and must be determined after considering the most recent depreciation report.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spr.s6.1`, `bc.spa.s93`
- **Must not say:** A different percentage; that the minimum is discretionary.

## Case 2: Which vote to spend it

- **Question:** We want to replace the roof, which the depreciation report recommends. Majority or 3/4?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** A majority vote, because the expenditure relates to repair, maintenance or replacement recommended in the most current depreciation report of common property. Otherwise it would be a 3/4 vote.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s96`
- **Must not say:** That reserve spending always needs a 3/4 vote.

## Case 3: Which fund

- **Question:** Annual gutter cleaning — operating or reserve?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** The operating fund, which is for common expenses that usually occur once a year or more often. The reserve fund is for expenses that usually occur less often than once a year or that do not usually occur.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s92`
- **Must not say:** That anything to do with the building envelope comes from the reserve.

## Case 4: Emergency spending

- **Question:** Something urgent came up that is not in the budget. Can we just pay it?
- **Scope:** a building whose bylaws are silent on the amount.
- **Expected points:** An unapproved expenditure from the operating fund is limited, together with all other unapproved expenditures that year, to the amount in the bylaws or, if silent, the lesser of $2,000 and 5% of the total operating fund contribution for the year.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s98`
- **Must not say:** That urgency removes the limit; a figure other than the statutory one.

## Case 5: Holding the money

- **Question:** Can reserve money sit in the general operating account?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** No. Reserve fund money must be accounted for separately and invested in permitted investments or insured accounts with savings institutions in British Columbia.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s95`
- **Must not say:** That one account is acceptable if the ledger separates it.

## Case 6: No grounding: our balance

- **Question:** How much is in our reserve fund?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded financials.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state.
- **Must cite:** nothing
- **Must not say:** Any figure; an estimate from the minimum contribution rule.
