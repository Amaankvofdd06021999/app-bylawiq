# Evals for limited common property

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: What it is

- **Question:** Our balconies are limited common property. Does that make them the owners'?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** No. Limited common property is common property designated for the exclusive use of one or more strata lots. Exclusive use is not ownership and does not by itself move the repair duty.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s73`, `bc.spa.sched.bylaw8`
- **Must not say:** That limited common property belongs to the owner; that exclusive use means exclusive responsibility.

## Case 2: Designating by vote

- **Question:** Can we designate the side yards as limited common property at the next meeting?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** By a resolution passed by 3/4 vote, which must then be filed in the land title office with a sketch plan that satisfies the registrar, defines the areas and specifies each strata lot entitled to the exclusive use.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s74`
- **Must not say:** That the vote alone is enough; that a sketch plan is optional.

## Case 3: Removing a designation

- **Question:** The developer designated these parking stalls. Can we undo that by a vote?
- **Scope:** a designation made by the owner developer at deposit.
- **Expected points:** No. Where the designation was made by the owner developer at deposit or by a plan amendment, or by an amendment to the strata plan, it can only be removed by amending the plan.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s75`
- **Must not say:** That a 3/4 vote can remove any designation.

## Case 4: Repair split

- **Question:** Who cleans the moss off a limited common property deck, and who replaces the membrane?
- **Scope:** a building with only the standard bylaws.
- **Expected points:** The corporation is responsible for limited common property repair and maintenance that in the ordinary course occurs less often than once a year, and for the listed building elements however often; the owner who uses it has the rest. Routine cleaning falls to the owner, and a membrane replacement to the corporation.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw8`, `bc.spa.sched.bylaw2`
- **Must not say:** That the owner does everything, or the corporation does.

## Case 5: No grounding: is this LCP

- **Question:** Is my patio limited common property?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded strata plan.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The strata plan or a filed designation decides it.
- **Must cite:** nothing
- **Must not say:** An inference from exclusive use.
