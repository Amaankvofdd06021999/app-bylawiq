# Evals for general meetings and voting

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: Notice period

- **Question:** How much notice for an annual general meeting?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** At least two weeks' written notice, to every owner, to every mortgagee who has filed a Mortgagee's Request for Notification, and to every tenant assigned a landlord's right to vote where the corporation has notice; four weeks where a winding-up resolution will be considered.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s45`
- **Must not say:** Any other period; omitting mortgagees or assigned tenants.

## Case 2: Quorum

- **Question:** Only 20% of owners turned up. Can we proceed?
- **Scope:** a building with only the standard bylaws.
- **Expected points:** No business may be conducted without a quorum, which subject to the bylaws is eligible voters holding 1/3 of the votes, present in person or by proxy, or 2/3 where there are fewer than 4 lots or owners.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s48`
- **Must not say:** That business can proceed and be ratified later.

## Case 3: Amending a resolution from the floor

- **Question:** Can we reword the 3/4 vote resolution at the meeting?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Only if the amendment does not substantially change the resolution and is itself approved by a 3/4 vote before the vote on the resolution.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s50`
- **Must not say:** That any amendment is allowed; that a majority can amend a 3/4 resolution.

## Case 4: A narrow 3/4 vote

- **Question:** The 3/4 vote passed but only 40% of all votes were cast in favour. Can we start work?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Not for one week, unless there are reasonable grounds to believe immediate action is necessary to ensure safety or prevent significant loss or damage. Within that week, holders of at least 25% of the votes may demand a special general meeting to reconsider.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s51`
- **Must not say:** That the resolution takes effect immediately.

## Case 5: Secret ballot

- **Question:** An owner wants a secret ballot for the council election. Must we?
- **Scope:** a building with the standard bylaws.
- **Expected points:** Yes. Despite the rest of the voting bylaw, an election of council or any other vote must be by secret ballot if an eligible voter requests it.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw27`
- **Must not say:** That the chair may refuse; that it applies only to council elections.

## Case 6: No grounding: was the amendment filed

- **Question:** Did our 2019 bylaw amendment ever get filed?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded title search or filed bylaws.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The answer says an amendment has no effect until filed and that the filing record is not available.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s128`
- **Must not say:** An assumption either way.
