# Evals for alterations and renovations

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: Refusing approval

- **Question:** Can council just say no to an alteration inside a unit?
- **Scope:** a building with only the standard bylaws.
- **Expected points:** Not without a reason. The standard bylaw says approval must not be unreasonably withheld for the listed strata lot alterations, though it may be made conditional on the owner taking responsibility in writing for the expenses.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw5`
- **Must not say:** That council has an unfettered discretion; that the same duty applies to common property alterations, where the standard bylaw has no equivalent wording.

## Case 2: Common property alterations

- **Question:** An owner wants to enclose their balcony. What approval is needed?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Written approval under the common property alteration bylaw, and separately a check whether the change is a significant change in the use or appearance of common property, which needs a 3/4 vote.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw6`, `bc.spa.s71`
- **Must not say:** That council approval alone is always enough.

## Case 3: Who repairs it afterwards

- **Question:** If we approve an alteration, does the owner become responsible for repairing it forever?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Only so far as the Act allows the duty to be shifted, and so far as the agreement says. A condition about the expenses of the alteration and a permanent shift of the repair duty are different things.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s72`, `bc.spa.sched.bylaw5`
- **Must not say:** That an alteration agreement can move any repair duty; that it automatically binds later owners.

## Case 4: Bare land plans

- **Question:** Does the alteration bylaw apply in our bare land strata?
- **Scope:** a bare land strata plan with only the standard bylaws.
- **Expected points:** The standard bylaw on altering a strata lot does not apply to a strata lot in a bare land strata plan. The common property alteration bylaw is not limited that way.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw5`, `bc.spa.sched.bylaw6`
- **Must not say:** That the strata lot alteration bylaw applies in a bare land plan.

## Case 5: No grounding: is this alteration approved

- **Question:** Was the kitchen renovation in unit 8 approved?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded minutes or alteration agreements.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The answer says the approval record is not available.
- **Must cite:** nothing
- **Must not say:** An inference from the work having been done.
