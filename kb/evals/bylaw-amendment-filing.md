# Evals for bylaw amendment and filing

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: When an amendment takes effect

- **Question:** We passed the amendment at the AGM in March. Can we enforce it now?
- **Scope:** a building that has not filed the amendment.
- **Expected points:** No. An Amendment to Bylaws in the prescribed form must be filed in the land title office, and until that filing the amendment has no effect.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s128`
- **Must not say:** That the vote is what matters; that enforcement can start pending filing.

## Case 2: New buildings

- **Question:** Can a new residential strata amend its bylaws by 3/4 vote before the second AGM?
- **Scope:** a wholly residential or bare land strata plan before its second annual general meeting.
- **Expected points:** No. No amendment may be made before the second annual general meeting unless approved by a unanimous vote.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s127`
- **Must not say:** That the ordinary 3/4 vote applies.

## Case 3: Mixed-use buildings

- **Question:** We have shops on the ground floor. What vote amends the bylaws?
- **Scope:** a strata plan of both residential and nonresidential strata lots.
- **Expected points:** Both a 3/4 vote of the residential strata lots and a 3/4 vote of the nonresidential strata lots, or as otherwise provided in the bylaws for the nonresidential lots.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s128`
- **Must not say:** A single 3/4 vote of all owners.

## Case 4: Policies

- **Question:** Council adopted a renovation policy. Is it enforceable like a bylaw?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Not as a bylaw. A bylaw needs the vote and the filing; a rule is a different instrument limited to common property and common assets and needs ratification. A policy that is neither cannot be enforced as one.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s128`, `bc.spa.s125`
- **Must not say:** That a council policy binds owners as a bylaw does.

## Case 5: No grounding: which version is filed

- **Question:** What do our filed bylaws currently say about pets?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded bylaws or title search.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The answer may say the standard bylaws apply to the extent nothing different is filed, and must not state this building's position.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s120`
- **Must not say:** A claim about this building's filed bylaws.
