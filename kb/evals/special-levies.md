# Evals for special levies

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: Which vote

- **Question:** We want to split a levy by unit rather than unit entitlement. What vote do we need?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** A unanimous vote. A 3/4 vote suffices only where each lot's share is calculated by the statutory formula; any other fair division needs unanimity.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s108`
- **Must not say:** That a 3/4 vote is enough for any levy.

## Case 2: Selling mid-levy

- **Question:** A lot sold halfway through a levy. Who owes the rest?
- **Scope:** a levy approved before the conveyance.
- **Expected points:** The seller owes the portion payable before the conveyance date and the buyer owes the portion payable on or after it.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s109`
- **Must not say:** That the whole levy follows the seller, or the buyer; that a private agreement changes what the Act provides as between the owners and the corporation.

## Case 3: Unpaid levy

- **Question:** Can an unpaid special levy go on a lien?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Yes, a special levy is one of the amounts a Certificate of Lien may secure, after the two weeks' written demand.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s116`, `bc.spa.s112`
- **Must not say:** That only strata fees can be liened.

## Case 4: Deductible levy

- **Question:** Do we need a vote for a levy to pay the insurance deductible?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** No. Approval is not required for a special levy to cover an insurance deductible the corporation must pay to repair or replace damaged property, unless it has decided not to repair or replace.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s158`
- **Must not say:** That a 3/4 vote is required.

## Case 5: No grounding: what the levy was for

- **Question:** What was the 2023 levy in this building spent on?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded minutes or financials.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state.
- **Must cite:** nothing
- **Must not say:** An inference from the amount.
