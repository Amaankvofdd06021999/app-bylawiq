# Evals for repair and maintenance

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: The default division

- **Question:** Who repairs the window in a strata lot?
- **Scope:** a building with only the standard bylaws, not a bare land plan.
- **Expected points:** The corporation, because the standard bylaw makes it responsible for doors, windows and skylights on the exterior of a building or fronting on common property, within a strata lot. The owner's duty covers the rest of the lot.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw8`, `bc.spa.sched.bylaw2`
- **Must not say:** That the owner is responsible because the window is inside their lot.

## Case 2: Shifting repair onto owners

- **Question:** Can we pass a bylaw making owners repair the common property corridors on their floor?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Not unless the Regulation identifies that common property, and subject to the prescribed restrictions. The corporation may shift limited common property the owner may use, and may take on portions of a strata lot, but shifting other common property is limited.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s72`
- **Must not say:** That a 3/4 vote is all that is needed.

## Case 3: Work orders

- **Question:** The city sent us an order about a unit. What do we do?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Promptly give the notice or order to the owner, who must do the work. If the owner does not, the corporation may do it; it must wait for an appeal if told of one in writing; and except in an emergency it must give a week's written notice before starting.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s84`, `bc.spa.s85`
- **Must not say:** That the corporation can do the work immediately; that it can ignore an appeal.

## Case 4: Doing the work and charging for it

- **Question:** Can we fix something an owner is refusing to fix and bill them?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Where it is a contravention of the bylaws or rules, the corporation may do what is reasonably necessary to remedy it and require the reasonable costs from the person who could be fined — after the complaint and answer process.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s133`, `bc.spa.s135`
- **Must not say:** That the corporation may do work and invoice without process.

## Case 5: No grounding: our bylaws' division

- **Question:** Who repairs the patio doors in this building?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded bylaws.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state for this building. The answer may set out the standard bylaw default and must say the building's own bylaws are not available and may differ.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.sched.bylaw8`
- **Must not say:** A conclusion presented as this building's position.
