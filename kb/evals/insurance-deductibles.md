# Evals for insurance and deductibles

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: What must be insured

- **Question:** What does the strata corporation have to insure?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Common property, common assets, the buildings shown on the strata plan, and fixtures built or installed on a strata lot by the owner developer as part of the original construction, generally at full replacement value. The fixtures limb does not apply to a bare land strata plan.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s149`
- **Must not say:** That the corporation insures the contents of strata lots; that owners' improvements are covered.

## Case 2: Named insureds

- **Question:** Is the owner covered by our policy?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Named insureds include the corporation, the owners and tenants from time to time, and the persons who normally occupy the strata lots, despite the terms of the policy.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s155`
- **Must not say:** That only the corporation is insured; a statement about what the policy in fact covers.

## Case 3: Telling owners about a deductible increase

- **Question:** Our deductible went from $25,000 to $100,000. Do we have to tell owners?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Yes, as soon as feasible: the corporation must inform owners and tenants of any material change in coverage, including an increase in a deductible, and must report on coverage at each annual general meeting.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s154`
- **Must not say:** That it can wait for the next annual general meeting.

## Case 4: Charging the deductible

- **Question:** Can we charge the deductible to the owner whose unit the leak came from?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Not automatically. It is a common expense contributed to through strata fees; the corporation may sue to recover it where the owner is responsible for the loss or damage, and a bylaw may create a chargeback. Responsibility has to be established.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s158`
- **Must not say:** That the Act makes the owner liable; that a chargeback bylaw needs no proof of responsibility.

## Case 5: Deciding not to repair

- **Question:** Can we take the insurance money and not rebuild?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Only by a 3/4 vote at a general meeting held no later than 60 days after receipt of the insurance money, after which the money is held in trust and distributed according to each person's interest or as a court orders.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s159`
- **Must not say:** That council can decide; any period other than 60 days.

## Case 6: No grounding: what our policy covers

- **Question:** Does our policy cover sewer backup?
- **Scope:** a building with no uploaded policy.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The answer can say what the Act requires to be insured and must not state what this policy covers.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s149`
- **Must not say:** A statement about this building's coverage.
