# Evals for move-in and move-out

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: A fee in an unratified rule

- **Question:** Our move-in fee is in a rule that was never ratified. Can we charge it?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** No. A user fee must be set out in a bylaw or in a rule that has been ratified, and an unratified rule ceases to have effect at the first annual general meeting after it is made.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s110`, `bc.spr.s6.9`, `bc.spa.s125`
- **Must not say:** That a posted fee schedule is enough.

## Case 2: How much is reasonable

- **Question:** Is a $500 move-in fee allowed?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** The Regulation requires the amount to be reasonable and allows it to be fixed or set on a reasonable basis such as recovery of operating or maintenance costs, duration of use or the number of users. The kb cannot say whether a particular figure is reasonable.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spr.s6.9`
- **Must not say:** A maximum dollar figure, which the Regulation does not set; a conclusion that $500 is or is not reasonable.

## Case 3: A deposit against damage

- **Question:** Can we hold a damage deposit for a move?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** A charge for actual damage is neither a user fee nor a fine. Where a deposit is applied as a penalty for a contravention, the complaint and answer process applies.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s135`, `bc.spr.s6.9`
- **Must not say:** That a deposit can be kept without accounting for actual damage.

## Case 4: Screening through the move-in form

- **Question:** Can the move-in form ask for the tenant's references?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** No, so far as it amounts to screening tenants or requiring approval of them; the corporation must not do that. Collecting the information the Act itself requires about a tenancy is different.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s141`
- **Must not say:** That a move-in form is outside section 141 because it is administrative.

## Case 5: No grounding: our building's fee

- **Question:** What is the move-in fee here?
- **Scope:** a building that has uploaded no bylaws or rules.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state.
- **Must cite:** nothing
- **Must not say:** A typical fee; a figure from another building.
