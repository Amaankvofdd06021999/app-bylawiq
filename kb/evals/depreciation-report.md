# Evals for depreciation reports

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: How often

- **Question:** How often do we need a depreciation report?
- **Scope:** a strata plan with 5 or more strata lots.
- **Expected points:** At least once every 5 years, with transitional deadlines for corporations established before 1 July 2024 and separate first-report deadlines for newer corporations running from the first annual general meeting.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spr.s6.21`, `bc.spa.s94`
- **Must not say:** That the requirement can be waived by a 3/4 vote each year.

## Case 2: Small buildings

- **Question:** We have 4 strata lots. Do we need one?
- **Scope:** a strata plan with fewer than 5 strata lots.
- **Expected points:** No. The requirement does not apply for so long as there are fewer than 5 strata lots in the strata plan.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spr.s6.22`
- **Must not say:** That every strata corporation must have one.

## Case 3: Who can write it

- **Question:** Can a local contractor prepare it?
- **Scope:** a report obtained on or after 1 July 2025.
- **Expected points:** Only if they are in one of the listed professional categories and have the knowledge and expertise to understand the components, scope and complexity of the property the corporation must maintain or repair.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spr.s6.2`
- **Must not say:** That any experienced person qualifies.

## Case 4: Drafts and records requests

- **Question:** An owner wants the draft report. Must we produce it?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** The records the corporation must retain include any depreciation reports obtained under the Act, and the right of access runs to those records. The answer should note that the retention duty is expressed as any reports obtained, and should not resolve a contested characterisation of a particular document.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s35`, `bc.spa.s36`
- **Must not say:** A flat refusal on the basis that a draft is not a report.

## Case 5: No grounding: when ours is due

- **Question:** When is our next depreciation report due?
- **Scope:** a building that has not uploaded its last report or its establishment date.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The answer sets out the cycle and the transitional deadlines and says the dates for this building are not available.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spr.s6.21`
- **Must not say:** A specific date for this building.
