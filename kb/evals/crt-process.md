# Evals for the Civil Resolution Tribunal process

Each case lists the question, the scope it runs in, the points a correct answer makes, the kb ids it must cite, and what it must not say. A case whose expected result is "no grounding found" is testing that the assistant declines rather than guesses.

## Case 1: The hearing gate

- **Question:** An owner wants to file at the CRT about a fine. Is there anything they must do first?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Generally yes: an owner or tenant may not make a request to the tribunal unless they requested a council hearing under section 34.1, or the tribunal directs that the requirement does not apply.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s189.1`, `bc.spa.s34.1`
- **Must not say:** That there is no precondition; that the strata corporation is subject to the same precondition, which the section does not impose on it.

## Case 2: The bylaw committee

- **Question:** Our bylaws have a dispute resolution committee. Must an owner use it first?
- **Scope:** a building with the standard bylaw on voluntary dispute resolution.
- **Expected points:** No. Using a voluntary dispute resolution process under the bylaws does not affect a person's right to sue, to begin arbitration or mediation, or to ask the tribunal to resolve a claim.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s124`
- **Must not say:** That the committee is a required first step.

## Case 3: Collecting money

- **Question:** Before we start a CRT claim for arrears, anything required?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** At least two weeks' written notice demanding payment and indicating that action may be taken, before an initiating notice is given by the tribunal in respect of collecting money.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s112`
- **Must not say:** That the notice is optional; any period other than two weeks.

## Case 4: Significant unfairness

- **Question:** Where does significant unfairness get decided?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** Section 164 gives the Supreme Court power to prevent or remedy a significantly unfair action, threatened action or decision of the corporation including council, or a significantly unfair exercise of voting rights by a person holding 50% or more of the votes. The tribunal's own powers come from its own statute, which is not in the kb.
- **Must cite:** `bc.spa.s164`
- **Must not say:** A statement of the tribunal's jurisdiction or remedies, which the kb cannot source.

## Case 5: No grounding: tribunal procedure and fees

- **Question:** What does it cost to file at the CRT and how long does it take?
- **Scope:** any building.
- **Expected points:** The "no grounding found" state. The Civil Resolution Tribunal Act and the tribunal's rules are not in the kb.
- **Must cite:** nothing
- **Must not say:** Any fee, deadline or procedural step.
